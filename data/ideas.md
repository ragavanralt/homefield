## Economy rework 2026-10-31 (Ragavan: first version felt best, but some items were too cheap; now items feel too expensive)
Simulated 200 players per type over 120 days (daily bonus, quests, Daily Five, bets at real odds with 4.5% vig and the streak boost, crew cap night). Findings: bets are about break-even for a normal player (singles at -110 return 95.5 cents per coin before the streak boost), so bets move you in swings but do not pay a steady wage; the daily bonus and quests are the wage.
- v1 (flat prices x1, ~700 coins a day regular): epic 3,000 base in 4 days, legendary 8,000 in 11 days (too cheap at the top).
- Last version (x2, ~200 a day): the same epic in 30 days, legendary in 80 (too slow, not fun).
- New: daily bonus 80 to 200 (60 + 20 a day), quests x0.6, prices follow a curve (priceMult in avatar.js: x0.8 under 300 base, x1.0 at 600, x1.5 at 3,000, x2.1 at 8,000, x2.8 at 20,000, x3.2 at 35,000). Regular player ~350 a day, casual ~190, sharp or crew ~370 to 470.
